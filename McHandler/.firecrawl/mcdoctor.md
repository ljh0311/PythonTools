## FAQ

How do I analyze a Minecraft crash log?

Upload your latest.log or crash report. MCDoctor AI automatically reads the log, detects issues, explains the cause, and gives a fix.

Does MCDoctor support Paper, Fabric, Forge, or Spigot servers?

Yes. MCDoctor supports Paper, Purpur, Spigot, Bukkit, Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge, and Vanilla logs.

What types of errors can MCDoctor detect?

Mod/plugin conflicts, missing dependencies, corrupted chunks, Java errors, Watchdog freezes, configuration issues, out-of-memory crashes, and more.

Is my log file safe?

Your log is analyzed securely and never stored permanently. No personal data is kept.
